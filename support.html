Frequently asked questions

How do I start a dinner party?

Sign in, tap New Party, give it a name, and share the 6-character code (or invite link) with your group. Everyone who joins can add restaurants and vote.

How does voting work?

Swipe right on restaurants you'd eat at, left on ones you wouldn't (on desktop, use the buttons). When everyone in the party has voted, restaurants that got a yes from the whole group show up as matches.

Why can't I sign in?

Make sure you're using a Google or Apple account and have an internet connection. If sign-in fails with an error, email us the exact message and your device model so we can fix it.

How do I leave a dinner party?

On the home screen, tap the trash icon on the party and confirm. Your votes and membership are removed immediately.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, scroll to the bottom of your dinner-party list, and tap Delete account. See Delete Your Account for details on what data is removed.
